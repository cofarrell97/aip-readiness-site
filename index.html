How mature is your organisation's investment decision-making?

This short assessment gives you an independent read on your organisation's maturity in asset investment planning and value-based decision-making, benchmarked against a five-level scale drawn from ISO 55000 and the Institute of Asset Management's maturity model.

It is written for people who own or shape capital and asset investment decisions. There are no right answers: each question asks you to pick the description that most closely matches how your organisation actually works today, not how you would like it to work.
